## Criticisms addressed

The previous narrative moved too quickly from early classifiers to E92. The revision
adds the external-detector stage, failed source transfer, specialist combinations and
rejected correction attempts. It explains the engineering choices without pretending
that every experiment was a separate discovery or an independent test.

The report previously under-explained the role of documentation. Section 4.3 now describes
how the plan set the question, the experiment log retained results, the history recorded
the decision and the dataset register controlled admissible use. The reversed-label case
shows a concrete change of conclusion. This supports the documented 40-day project scope
without inventing a day-by-day attendance narrative.

Several passages sounded like an internal audit rather than a student explaining work.
The revision replaces long raw thresholds and dense implementation language with plain
score and runtime explanations. It defines parent images and data roles before numerical
comparisons. Repeated model numbers remain where they help trace a claim; they are not
used as a substitute for an explanation.

The industry-example requirement needed explicit coverage. Section 3.4 now cites Adobe's
Content Credentials as a contrasting approach to the same trust problem. It is clearly
a report-time literature comparison; no implementation or internship-time adoption is
claimed. Fourteen references include ten scholarly works.

Model2 needed more than the latest small pilot. The revision retains the misleading early
mask-size procedure, the 120-image CocoGlide central-region baseline and shared COCO
origins before the sixteen-parent placement experiment. The report does not turn attractive
heatmaps or many correlated versions into independent evidence.

A literal seventeen-decimal threshold was unnecessary in the main narrative. Rounded
0–100 score thresholds now convey the UI meaning; exact values stay in project records.
Model2's comparison table uses two decimal AUC values. Extra precision in Appendix 9.1
retains the distinction near AUC 1.00 rather than rounding it to apparent perfection.

The E49 failure, one reference-relative E92 miss, E102's smaller false-alert count without
promotion, gallery dependence and unopened-but-not-yet-independent reserves remain visible.
Simpler language has not removed inconvenient results. No claim is made that rewritten
prose can prove human authorship or evade an AI detector. The student must understand and
confirm the account they submit.
